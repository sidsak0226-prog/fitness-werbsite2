🍎 Nutrition Guide

Master your diet — understand nutrients, calculate macros, and eat smart!

💪 Protein

Builds and repairs muscles. Sources: chicken, eggs, fish, beans, tofu, whey protein.

🍚 Carbohydrates

Main energy source. Choose complex carbs like oats, brown rice, and sweet potatoes.

🥑 Healthy Fats

Supports brain and hormone function. Found in nuts, olive oil, avocados, fatty fish.

💧 Hydration

Drink 3–4 liters of water daily. Stay hydrated to support recovery and performance.

🍊 Vitamins & Minerals

Essential for immunity and recovery. Eat colorful fruits and vegetables daily.

💡 Nutrition Tips

  • Eat every 3–4 hours to maintain steady energy levels.
  • Include protein in every meal to support muscle recovery.
  • Limit processed foods and sugary drinks.
  • Plan meals ahead to avoid unhealthy snacking.
  • Track your calorie intake if you have specific fitness goals.
